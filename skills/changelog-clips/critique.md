# Changelog clips: critique

Judge each clip independently, sound off, in every delivered shape and at 358 px. Read source claims separately after a blind retell. Inspect key frames, stress and phone sheets, every UI-change strip at full size, every decoded MP4 frame around press/change/release/reset, and the encoded final/first boundary. A sheet alone cannot approve motion.

Score 1–10; every category must reach 8:

1. **Clarity:** one change, its action and its consequence are understood on one viewing with adjacent release text. Social carries its own concise context.
2. **Truth:** actual app states, honest release/date, complete native action, correct sample values and causal order. A queued state is not success.
3. **Native motion:** pressed at contact, real hover until exit; actual app animation played completely at its own timing, or instant only where truly instant. No fades between captures, patches or synthetic row movement.
4. **Readability:** critical native label/result about 12 px at 358 px wide; all complete names/prices needed for the claim read. Film titles/reset labels fit; no screenshot softness or clipped glyphs.
5. **Composition:** whole native enclosure, equal opposite padding, one quiet product ground, stationary social title outside UI, fixed camera and scale in every shape.
6. **Pacing:** setup can be read, pointer rests before pressing, action completes, result holds for at least a second (reference over four). No dead wait hiding missing evidence.
7. **Replay:** reset clearly announced before restore, label persists through the end; no apparent undo, new transaction or new search on each replay. Poster holds the result without the reset label.
8. **Craft and delivery:** real product typography/colour, crisp edges, deterministic/fresh/stress checks, silent MP4/WebM without audio, correct dimensions/duration, reduced-motion poster and usable play/pause. Optional audio fitted cleanly at ≤ −1 dBTP.

Record every fault as clip, shape, time and frame number, what is wrong and its consequence. Sort it into reusable **code** (fix renderer/reference, recheck reference) or **text** (add precise guidance here and in style.md). Never repair a proof film by hand. Rerun only after fixing the skill; stop after two failed correction rounds and report what remains. Never lower a floor or loosen a check for a pass.

A passed drawn-copy stress test does not cancel a failed native-size selfCheck. A documented readability limitation is a held item, not a completed social deliverable. The builder and exporter must stop on that failure. If every actual native view is too dense, report that the product cannot support this clip grammar at phone size; a truthful hold is preferable to fake proof.

Check the delivery manifest's `held` reasons and `status`: a shape may be explicitly held while compatible shapes pass. Missing requested shapes make the family partial; they cannot be counted as a completed proof or silently removed from the score.

Check unattended completion as well as the files: finite captures, checks and exports must finish before the report.
An interactive preview server must not block the foreground delivery command; link the exported static player instead.

Inspect the resting pointer at phone width: it must not hide a critical letter during the approach/rest/press.
Any custom `targetFraction` must match the real mouse position used for native captures, inside the same actual hit area.

Compare the title's verb with the last retained state. A selected replay point cannot prove that a run replayed; a parsed expression cannot prove a schedule was saved. When the demonstration stops at a choice or preview, its title and adjacent copy must say so. A blind retell that infers execution is a clarity fault, even when every captured state is real.

## Independent motion review

Give a fresh reviewer the consecutive-frame strips and this brief, without story or builder conclusions:

> Frame-level motion review. Read every frame of every supplied strip. Look for abrupt starts/stops, cursor hover clearing before exit, presses that do not press or release, clipped glyphs, uneven native padding, old-state text, single-frame glitches, ghosts, moving rectangular patches, and reset labels arriving after the restore. An instant native change is valid; an invented fade is not. Report strip, frame, defect and expected behavior; skip clean strips. Score clarity, truth where visible, motion, readability, composition, pacing, replay and craft.

UI-change strips (`ui-<t>.png`) are every frame, full size, round one change in the product's UI. Look for a box or patch round anything that moves; text doubled or ghosted; a strip that slides on its own; a piece of page line/shadow carried with an item; a fade/dissolve between states; anything there for one frame.

For a blind retell, give only anonymized decoded video frames at phone width, in time order: “What changed? What caused it? What result remains? What happens at the end? Mark each sure, guessing or lost.” Resolve material guessing before declaring a pass.
