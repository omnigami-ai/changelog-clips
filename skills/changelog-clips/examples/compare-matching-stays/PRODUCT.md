# Ostlery

Ostlery is a fictional web assistant that finds small stays and books them, made for omnigami’s examples. Its stays, prices and people are invented sample data. URL: https://ostlery.example.

Brand: paper #F7F4EE, ink #17191F, dark surface #1C1916, Figtree (font and OFL in fonts/). The complete native panel is captured from the real app, never drawn for the film. Grounds inside its edges are sampled from captures.

Two release entries, approved 2026-11-06: Dark appearance (a saved local preference), and Compare matching stays (all six matches with their nightly prices). Neither sends a booking or changes the matching set. The sample resets after the result under an explicit Demo reset label.
